# SDK 0.3.2 Leather lower-fee Platform pin

## Context

The isolated Stacks testnet payer flow proposed and authorized an origin fee of `3000` micro-STX.
Leather preserved payer, nonce `0`, amount, recipient, memo and standard non-sponsored
authorization, but signed with a lower fee of `300` micro-STX. SDK 0.3.1 rejected the safe
reduction after canonical payment verification and before settlement. Two short-lived quotes were
issued; zero settlements or broadcasts occurred.

SDK PR #14 was reviewed and merged as `4d55848dc023bc3b0cfdd9d0a905006387d2d7d2` and released as
0.3.2. It treats the intent fee as a strict upper bound: only a positive verified origin fee less
than or equal to the intent is accepted. Fee increases and zero remain failures. All non-fee
payment fields remain exact, and policy accounting reserves the full maximum while committing the
verified actual fee.

## Change boundary

Platform 0.4.3 pins the exact public SDK 0.3.2 artifact and updates the verifier evidence persisted
with a settlement. The checksum remains the SHA-256 digest of the exact npm integrity string.
Service discovery reports Platform 0.4.3.

No contract, database schema, merchant policy, quote price, fee recommendation, settlement state
machine, route or runtime flag changes. Production remains quote-only: verification, settlement,
reconciliation, delivery and sponsorship stay disabled. The isolated E2E alone enables its
existing testnet gates.
